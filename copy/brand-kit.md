## 3. Brand Positioning

Lisa's Studio is the **warm, personal alternative to the big-box gym** — a small, welcoming studio in a natural woodland setting on Martha's Vineyard where people learn *how their bodies actually work* and build strength, mobility, and confidence with real one-on-one attention.

**Where it sits in the market:**
- **Not** a crowded commercial gym, a punishing bootcamp, or an anonymous chain.
- **Is** a boutique, education-first studio led by a single trusted, AFAA-certified instructor with 35+ years of experience.
- The value is **personal guidance and understanding**, not equipment or membership tiers.

**One-line positioning statement:**
> For people who don't like the gym, Lisa's Studio is the friendly Martha's Vineyard studio where expert, one-on-one instruction makes movement feel approachable — so you understand your body and actually enjoy taking care of it.

---

## 4. Target Audience

**Primary:** Adults on Martha's Vineyard (year-round residents and seasonal) who want to get or stay fit but feel out of place, bored, or intimidated at a traditional gym.

**Core segments:**
- **Gym-avoiders** — people who've tried gyms and bounced off; want guidance, not a treadmill.
- **Active agers (40s–70s+)** — focused on strength, balance, mobility, and staying independent.
- **Beginners & comeback clients** — returning after injury, illness, pregnancy, or a long break; need a safe, patient start.
- **Wellness-minded locals** — value education, personal attention, and a calm, natural setting over hype.
- **Small-group friends** — neighbors who prefer working out together in a low-key class.

**What they want:** to feel welcomed, to understand *why* they're doing each movement, to be seen as an individual, and to see steady, sustainable progress.

---

## 5. Brand Personality

Warm, approachable, and grounded — a knowledgeable friend, not a drill sergeant.

**Personality traits:**
- **Warm** — friendly, human, encouraging.
- **Approachable** — no jargon, no intimidation, no ego.
- **Knowledgeable** — 35+ years of expertise, education-first.
- **Grounded / natural** — calm, woodland setting; unhurried.
- **Personal** — you're a name, not a membership number.
- **Honest** — plain-spoken, real expectations, real results.

**Voice & tone:**
- Speak like a caring, experienced coach talking one-on-one.
- Use "you" and "we." Warm, plain, encouraging.
- Explain the *why* simply ("the more you understand how your body works, the better you move").
- **Avoid:** hype, shame, hardcore fitness clichés ("no pain no gain," "beast mode"), medical jargon.

## 7. Typography Direction

Modern, clean, and warm — a friendly serif for headings paired with a highly readable humanist sans for body. Both free from Google Fonts.

- **Headings — `Lora`** (serif): warm, calm, approachable; adds a personal, non-corporate feel. Weights 500–700.
- **Body & UI — `Raleway`** (sans-serif): clean, modern, friendly, excellent readability. Weights 300–600.

**Google Fonts import:**
```
@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Lora:wght@500;600;700&family=Raleway:wght@300;400;500;600&display=swap');
```

**Type scale (desktop):**

| Level | Font | Size / Line height | Weight |
|-------|------|--------------------|--------|
| Display / H1 | Lora | 48–60px / 1.1 | 700 |
| H2 | Lora | 32–40px / 1.2 | 600 |
| H3 | Lora | 24px / 1.3 | 600 |
| Body Large | Raleway | 18–20px / 1.6 | 400 |
| Body | Raleway | 16px / 1.6 | 400 |
| Label / Button | Raleway | 15–16px / 1.2 | 600 |
| Caption | Raleway | 13–14px / 1.4 | 500 |

**Rules:** body minimum 16px; line-height 1.5–1.75 for paragraphs; line length 60–75 characters; use weight (not just color) for hierarchy.

## 8. Logo Direction

A **fresh, minimal wordmark** that keeps the personal, trusted feel while modernizing the look.

**Concept — "Lisa's Studio" wordmark + leaf-in-motion mark:**
- **Wordmark:** "Lisa's Studio" set in **Lora** (500–600 weight), with the descriptor "Personal Training & Group Fitness" in **Raleway** small caps or letter-spaced caps beneath.
- **Symbol:** a single, simple line-drawn **leaf that doubles as a stride/movement curve** — one continuous stroke suggesting both nature (the woodland setting) and gentle motion (a body in movement). Minimal, one weight, drawn in Forest Moss or Terracotta.
- Alternative mark: a soft **"L" monogram** formed from the same single-stroke leaf/movement line.

**Usage:**
- **Primary lockup:** symbol left, wordmark right.
- **Stacked:** symbol above, wordmark below (for square/social avatars).
- **Wordmark only:** for tight spaces (nav bar, favicon uses the leaf/L mark alone).
- **Color:** Forest Moss on light backgrounds; Warm Sand/Cream on dark. Terracotta reserved for the symbol as an accent variant.
- **Clear space:** keep space equal to the cap-height of "L" on all sides.
- **Keep it simple:** one weight, no gradients, no drop shadows, scalable to a 32px favicon.

## 11. Visual Mood

**Direction:** warm, natural, and human. The website should feel like the studio itself — calm, welcoming, and unpretentious.

- **Lighting:** soft, warm, natural daylight — golden-hour tones, gentle window light, dappled light through trees. Never clinical or harsh.
- **Materials & textures:** natural wood, linen and cotton, greenery, warm neutral surfaces, soft mats. Subtle organic paper/canvas grain.
- **Backgrounds:** warm sand and cream fields; occasional deep-pine sections for contrast; soft, out-of-focus woodland and studio interiors.
- **Shapes:** rounded corners (16–24px), organic curves, gentle flowing dividers — nothing sharp or corporate.
- **Photography style:** real, candid, human moments — genuine coaching, real clients of varied ages and bodies, authentic smiles and effort. Warm color grade. Avoid stocky, over-posed "fitness model" imagery.
- **Motion:** slow, gentle, and purposeful — soft fades and easing (150–300ms), subtle parallax, calm reveals. Nothing flashy or high-energy.
- **Overall atmosphere:** approachable, grounded, restorative, trustworthy — the opposite of hardcore gym culture.
